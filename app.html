How to use ScreenShield

Protect a screen share

  1. Pick Source → Screen / tab and share the window you're about to present.
  2. ScreenShield OCRs it locally and pixelates emails, phones, cards, API keys, passwords and your custom keywords.
  3. Press Share-safe mirror (F) — the page becomes a clean full-screen mirror.
  4. In your meeting, share this ScreenShield tab instead of the original window. Viewers only ever see the redacted mirror.

Detectors

  • Email — standard address patterns.
  • Phone — international formats, 8–15 digits.
  • Card — 13–19 digit numbers that pass the Luhn checksum.
  • API secret — GitHub, Stripe, AWS, Slack, Google keys + generic high-entropy tokens.
  • Password — short value lines right after a “password” label (heuristic).
  • Keyword — anything on your custom list.

Honest limits

OCR can miss stylized, tiny or low-contrast text, and redaction lags fast-moving content by one scan interval. Always double-check the mirror before presenting. This is a safety net, not a guarantee.

Privacy

All OCR runs locally in WASM. Findings store masked previews only — raw values are never persisted, logged or exported.
